No mesh, no rig file, no motion capture. Why this counts as a rig

Each figure is a root, a torso lean, a head tilt, and four two-segment limbs — nine angles in, a pose out. That's a skeleton, just not one Unreal or Blender would recognize. Enough joints to carry weight, hesitation, and posture; not enough to need a mesh.

What it can't do

No face. No cloth, no hair, no finger detail, no camera close enough to read an expression. This reads at the 3/4 distance the brief asks for — it does not survive a push into a dialogue close-up. That gap is exactly where hand-keyed or mocap work still has to live.

Where this goes next

The pose math here (poseFor()) is the reusable part — it doesn't care if it's drawn with canvas strokes or driving bone transforms on a stylized low-poly rig in UE5. Proving the behavior in 2D first is the cheap way to find out if it reads before spending on a single triangle.
