House portraits — the Beauvais and the Rowe

Six bosses each, because the boss is drawn per game and a house is not one face. Same skin ramps, same builds, same ages on both racks; the names are the real pools out of config/houses.ts, which is why half of them are women. What tells the houses apart is the kit and the light, and both of those are the business showing.

The same two people, in either house the claim, shown rather than asserted
identical face — same skin ramp, age, build, hair, features — and nothing changed but the kit and the light

What this is not. Nothing here encodes a family as a face, and the row above is the check on that rather than a promise about it: the two pairs are the same person rendered twice, and what moved them between houses was a collar, a cap and where the sun was. That is the whole design. houses.ts says a house is a way of doing business, and if the art had answered that with two sets of features it would have quietly made the file a liar.
