Pixel fronts — the ten in businesses.ts

Not buildings somebody thought would be nice: these are the ten entries in config/businesses.ts, drawn. Every one of them already carries a field called legitimacy — “How unremarkable it looks” — so the brief for each facade was written in the config years before anyone drew it. The sheet is sorted by that number, 80 down to 25, and the only question a sprite has to answer is whether it looks as unremarkable as its number says.

The street

The same ten, tiled. Shells run to the party wall on both sides on purpose, so a district's holdings can be drawn as one terrace rather than a row of separate objects — which is also the honest test of the legitimacy sort: scan the row and the two you notice first are the two at the bottom of it.
