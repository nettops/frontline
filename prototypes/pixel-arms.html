Pixel armoury — the arms trade

Thirty-one pieces of 1978 hardware, drawn side-on in a fixed 48×16 cell, business end left. The reference sheet this came from sorts by damage; this one sorts two ways the game can actually use — what it costs to carry, and what it leaves behind afterwards. Three finishes, one shape each: the same trick the reference uses for black, silver and gold, pointed at provenance rather than rarity.

Three finishes, one sprite

Nothing below is redrawn. The grid holds palette keys and the finish supplies four hexes for the metal, which is the whole reason a sheet like this can afford variants at all.

Where it would live

Left: the arms trade already exists and its unit is a crate, so the crate is the only sprite here the simulation can currently ask for. Right: hardware as a line item, at the size a list would actually give it.

Palette

The metal ramp is the game's own --carbon and --carbon-dim — the cold institutional blue it uses for law enforcement. That was an accident of what was in the file and then it was not, because it is the right colour for steel and the right joke about it. Wood reuses the brown skin ramp from pixel-cast.html. Two new darks, and nothing else.
