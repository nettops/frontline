Pixel character mockups — the boss

He is not drawn, he is subtracted: a flat near-black mass against a lit wall, no face, no shading, one coal and its smoke. Three specs of the same man, because what is being decided is not what a boss looks like but what every character drawn after him is committed to. Nothing here needs a colour the game does not already have.

At 1x, where they would actually live

The first panel is the game as it stands, and it is the whole problem: a man made of shadow, on a surface made of shadow, is nothing. The second is the same sprite over one flat cold rectangle. That rectangle is the real ask.
