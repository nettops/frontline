Pixel rooms — the other side of the ten fronts

The inside of every facade in pixel-fronts.html, which is the inside of every entry in config/businesses.ts. 96 × 56, flat elevation, one wall and one floor line. The cell is that size for a specific reason, and the last section is it.

The sit-down

Nothing was redrawn to make this. The room is the social club above and the two figures are compose() output from pixel-cast.html, dropped in at y=8 — which puts the bottom of a 32 × 40 half-figure exactly where the table is. The character sheet and the building sheet were drawn six commits apart and they fit without either moving, because both of them were built to an anchor rather than to taste.
