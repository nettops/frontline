Crew — rank you can see, familiarity you have to earn

Two axes, both already in the simulation. Rank is seven values in sim/types.ts and is read off clothing, because rank is the thing about a man the player is always allowed to see. Familiarity is a number on every Npc, and memories.ts already refuses to show you what he is carrying below 55 of it. Every other system obeys that. The art did not — a new hire and a man you have run twenty jobs with were drawn identically. Here the sprite resolves as you earn it.

The ladder — seven ranks, read off the clothes

All at full familiarity, so only rank is varying. A half-figure cut at the waist cannot show an overcoat, so rank is carried by four things it can show: headgear (cap → fedora → homburg), collar (open → tie), build, and how good the cloth is — the suits get darker and deeper up the ladder, which is the one gradient that runs the whole length of it.

The same man, four times

One sprite, four palettes — nothing is redrawn below the top tier. The breakpoints are the game's own: STARTING_FAMILIARITY = 5, and RECALL.visibleAbove = 55, which is where memories.ts starts letting you see what he is holding. It is also where his face arrives.

The roster, mixed

Which is the point of the whole thing: you can see at a glance who you actually know. Two of these men are shapes. That is not a rendering limitation, it is the answer to “who would you trust with this”.
